# Community Guidelines & Code of Conduct

## Purpose & Values
DAYCRAFT is built to be a respectful, productive, and inclusive project for engineering students, developers, and habit-tracking enthusiasts. We are committed to maintaining a supportive environment where all contributors feel valued and empowered.

## Our Standards
We expect everyone participating in this project to uphold the following principles:
- **Mutual Respect**: Treat every participant with courtesy, kindness, and empathy regardless of background, identity, or experience level.
- **Constructive Communication**: Offer helpful, action-oriented feedback on code, designs, and architectural proposals.
- **Accountability**: Take ownership of mistakes gracefully, learn from team discussions, and prioritize the long-term health of the software.
- **Focus on the Community**: Prioritize solutions that benefit users and maintain the privacy and performance integrity of the app.

## Unacceptable Conduct
The following behaviors are strictly prohibited:
- Harassment, discriminatory language, insults, or personal attacks.
- Sharing private personal data of others without explicit consent.
- Unsolicited commercial advertising, spamming, or trolling.

## Reporting & Moderation
If you encounter conduct that violates these guidelines, please contact the maintainers via GitHub Issues or project discussions. Project maintainers review concerns promptly and will take appropriate corrective steps, including issuing warnings or restricting access to the repository when necessary.
